Best leaf blowers with pickup bags.

A useful shortlist for people who want leaves in a bag, not merely somewhere else in the yard.

UPDATED OCTOBER 20267 MIN READ
EDITORIAL ILLUSTRATION · NOT A LISTED MODEL

A blower moves leaves. A blower vac picks them up, shreds them, and keeps them in a bag. If cleanup is the whole job, that distinction matters.

How this guide was made

This is a specification-based shortlist, not a hands-on test or a performance ranking. We checked manufacturer pages for pickup mode, bag setup, power source, and stated features. Check current availability and the exact kit before buying.

The short answer

Choose a corded model if your cleanup area is close to an outlet and you value continuous runtime. Choose a cordless model if dragging a cable around beds, gates, and trees would make you avoid the job. In either case, check that the package includes the vacuum tubes and collection bag.

Three models worth comparing

01

BLACK+DECKER BEBL7000 VACPACK

BEST FIT: LONGER CORDED CLEANUP SESSIONS

This 12-amp corded blower, vacuum, and mulcher wears its collection bag as a backpack. BLACK+DECKER lists up to 250 mph air speed and 400 cfm airflow, plus a roll-and-clip bag opening designed for quicker emptying. The cord removes battery runtime anxiety; it also limits where you can work.

BEST FIT: CORDLESS CLEANUP

Toro UltraPlus 51621

BEST FIT: A TRADITIONAL CORDED BLOWER VAC

Toro describes the UltraPlus as a blower, high-speed vacuum, and leaf shredder. Its parts documentation identifies a vacuum tube and bag assembly. Compare this one if you prefer a conventional handheld corded setup; confirm the bag and tubes are included with the seller's package.

What matters more than the headline number

Bag design

A bag that is awkward to remove or empty makes a good motor feel like a bad purchase. Look at where it sits, how it closes, and whether a replacement is readily available.

Wet leaves and rough debris

Vacuum mode works best when debris is reasonably dry. Check the manual before feeding a machine sticks, gravel, or soggy leaf mats. A rake is still a useful tool.

The conversion step

Some machines switch modes with different tubes and fittings. If you will change from blowing to pickup several times in one session, look at that process before focusing on air-speed claims.

Our take

For a small or medium yard with easy outlet access, begin with the corded BEBL7000 or Toro UltraPlus. For a yard full of obstacles or distant cleanup spots, compare the cordless WORX. The right choice is the one whose power source, bag, and conversion process suit your routine.
